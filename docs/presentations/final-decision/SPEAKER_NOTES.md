# MayaBela — final decision meeting

**Audience:** school directors, administration staff, parent representatives, other leadership bodies  
**Length:** 22–28 minutes + live demo + questions  
**Open:** `docs/presentations/final-decision/index.html` in Chrome → **F11**  
**Keys:** Right / Space next · Left previous · click right half of screen = next

School owners have already seen MayaBela and directed execution. This room is the **operational yes**. Do not reopen the owner pitch from zero. Show each body *their* modules, then ask them to recommend.

Visuals in `screens/` are product-style module views for the projector. After the slides, open the **live** system: https://mayabela.pages.dev (Ctrl+Shift+R).

## Lines that close objections

- **“We already have SMS / Excel.”** Those digitize a file. MayaBela is governance: approve → publish, live bus, store trail, one login for every role.
- **“Parents will see everything.”** They see their child after the school approves the link. They never see unapproved grades.
- **“Teachers will be policed.”** Teachers keep the classroom tools. Leadership keeps publish rights. That protects the teacher as much as the parent.
- **“Is this a government system?”** No. It helps the school run digitally in line with Digital Ethiopia. Do not claim MoE endorsement.
- **“Our own server.”** Note the request; do not debate architecture in this room. Cloud pilot first. On-prem is a separate conversation after go-live.
- **Price:** Use the figure owners already heard (executive briefing: Essential 200 / Standard 300 / Campus+ 400 ETB per student + 15% VAT). If finance asks for a different model, say you will confirm with owners — do not undercut.
