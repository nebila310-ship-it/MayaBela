Fenote Raey Academy · Final decision meeting

What we present today

One school. Every role. One source of truth.
Presented by MaJo Bridge Technologies and Events

This meeting shows the school system Fenote Raey would run: office ERP plus the teacher, parent, student, and driver dashboards. No price discussion in this room. After the slides we open the live system.

Today’s walk — what you will see

  1. The problem — Excel, paper, and WhatsApp are not a school system.
  2. Who logs in — directors, student affairs, QA, teachers, students, parents, drivers. Same School ID, different homes.
  3. Student login — from the grade this school sets, not a fixed Grade 7.
  4. Directors & registrar — morning dashboard, student IDs, parent-link approval.
  5. Student affairs & alumni — discipline pipeline, leave, graduates stay in the book.
  6. The rule — teacher enters → leadership approves → then the parent sees.
  7. Classroom — teacher home, homework posting, student home, parent home.
  8. Student QR — one printed card for bus pick-up/drop-off, class attendance, and opening the student file by scan.
  9. Transport, fees, payroll, store — live bus, ETB collections, Ethiopian tax + pension, stock trail.
  10. School life & QA — announcements, gallery, calendar, messages, findings, improvement plans.
  11. Language & files — English / Amharic / Afaan Oromo. Cloud for daily work, plus a JSON copy on a school PC or school server.
  12. The ask — recommend a 30–60 day pilot on one campus or grade band.
