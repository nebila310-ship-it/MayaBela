## 1. The problem

Excel, paper, and WhatsApp are not a school system.

Directors hear problems late. Administration keeps rosters, fees, staff, and the store in different files. Parents wait for a call, a meeting, or a rumor — and sometimes they see a grade before leadership has approved it.

Most tools only help the registrar type faster. This one helps Fenote Raey sleep, because **one record is true for everyone**.

---

## 2. Who uses it

Look at this picture. Every person in this meeting has a home.

Same **School ID**. Different doors. Another school cannot open Fenote Raey. Staff only see the modules their duty requires.

Directors. Student affairs. Teachers. Quality assurance. Parents. Students. Drivers. Finance and store. If you are in this room, you are on this map.

---

## 3. Every dashboard

This is important. This is **not only the office ERP**.

Four homes. Teacher. Parent. Student. Driver. Each login opens a different home screen. Same School ID. Not one shared office menu.

If someone later says “this is only for the director,” come back to this picture.

---

## 4. Access

One login: **School ID plus phone or email**.

Web for the office. Phone for teachers, parents, students, and drivers. The first time you log in, the system forces a new password. Temporary passwords are shown once, then destroyed.

Languages already in the product: English, Amharic, and Afaan Oromo.

Amharic: **የትምህርት ቤት መታወቂያ + ስልክ ወይም ኢሜይል። የመጀመሪያ መግቢያ ላይ የይለፍ ቃል ይቀየራል።**

---

## 5. Student login

Students do **not** log in from a fixed Grade 7. **Fenote Raey sets the lowest grade** that may use the student app — Grade 1 to 12. Change it when the school is ready.

Below that grade, the parent app is the channel. Students still see **published grades only**. Homework upload, report download, messaging, class rank — those are separate switches the school can turn.

Amharic: **ተማሪ የሚገባው ትምህርት ቤቱ ከሚወስነው ክፍል ጀምሮ ነው።**

---

## 6. For school directors

This is command of the school in one screen.

Every morning: who is present, who is absent, open student-affairs cases, homework due, outstanding fees, live buses, QA follow-ups, gallery, calendar, unread messages. Reports export to Excel, CSV, or PDF.

The sidebar **is** the school: student affairs, alumni, classroom, quality, announcements, calendar, messages. Not three leftover tiles.

---

## 7. For administration — registrar

Students are a register, not a chat list.

Enroll with a short student ID. Date of birth is required so a parent can link safely. Transfers and promotion are a workflow — not a deleted Excel row. Parent requests wait for registrar or section director approval.

Wrong date of birth — no link. Approved link — the parent sees only their child.

---

## 8. For student affairs

Discipline and leave are a **desk**, not a rumour.

Behaviour cases have an owner — homeroom, student affairs, or VP. Parent leave requests wait for school approval. They do not vanish in a phone call. Teachers and parents see only what their duty allows. Open cases show on the director dashboard the same morning.

Amharic: **የተማሪ ጉዳይና የዲሲፕሊን መዝገብ በስርዓት ይቀመጣል።**

---

## 9. Student affairs — the pipeline

A case file has a beginning, a hearing, and a written end.

Report — time, place, students. Investigate — statements stay inside the school. Hearing — the parent is informed through the school channel. Outcome — warning, counselling, or further action, **on file**.

This is why student affairs is in the room today. Gossip is not a record.

---

## 10. Alumni records

Graduates stay in the school book.

When a class finishes, they are marked graduated. Transcript and certificate history is not a deleted row. Directors, registrar, and QA can still open the record. Alumni are not mixed back into today’s attendance.

---

## 11. Trust — say this slowly, twice

**Teacher enters. Leadership approves. Then the parent sees.**

This is the difference between a portal and a school.

Draft grades stay inside Fenote Raey. Section director, or the people you designate, publish. Parents never receive an unapproved mark. Attendance is saved by the teacher and visible in admin reports the same day.

Please remember this sentence. I will say it in Amharic:

**መምህር ያስገባል → አመራር ያጸድቃል → ከዚያ ወላጅ ያያል።**

---

## 12. For classroom teachers

Teachers, this is your home. Not the office menu.

My Classes. Attendance. Homework. Grades. Timetable. Gallery. Messages. Student affairs for what your duty allows. You do **not** get finance, payroll, or another campus’s children.

You enter the work. Leadership publishes. That protects you as much as it protects the parent.

Amharic: **የመምህር ዳሽቦርድ — ክፍሉ የሚያስፈልገው ሁሉ እዚህ ነው።**

---

## 13. Classroom management

Who teaches which class is not a hallway rumor.

Grades, sections, homeroom teacher, timetable. Directors see the roster in one list. Academic year is visible, so last year is not mixed with this year. Teachers then only see the classes they actually own.

---

## 14. Homework and assignments

Post the work. Parents see it. Teachers mark it done.

Title, due date, class, file. The parent dashboard shows the same homework for their child. Status moves from assigned to submitted or completed. We do not need “please WhatsApp the worksheet” for the whole class.

Amharic: **የቤት ሥራ በመተግበሪያ ይለጠፋል። ወላጅ ያያል።**

---

## 15. For students

If a student representative is here: this is **your** school, not the office.

Profile, published grades, homework, e-book, attendance, timetable, announcements, calendar, messages. You see your own work. You never see a draft mark.

Amharic: **የተማሪ ዳሽቦርድ — የራሱ ትምህርት ቤት፣ የቢሮው መዝገብ አይደለም።**

---

## 16. For parent representatives

Parents, you see **the child**, not the school’s internal politics.

Linked child, attendance, homework, grades, fees, bus tracking, behaviour and leave, messages. You link with **Student ID and date of birth**. Then the school approves. Until that approval, the door stays closed. You never see an unapproved mark.

Amharic: **ወላጅ የሚያየው ልጁን ብቻ ነው። ያልጸደቀ ውጤት አይታይም።**

## 17. Transport — driver dashboard

Drivers, this is your home on the road.

My Route. Live map. Passenger list. Scan QR. Pick-up and drop-off. Messages from the school. Parents see “live” **only while you are sharing location**. When sharing is off, it is not live.

Amharic: **የሹፌር ዳሽቦርድ — መስመር፣ ተሳፋሪ፣ QR እና ቀጥታ ካርታ።**

---

## 18. Transport — office ERP

The bus is part of the school, not a private WhatsApp.

Office assigns buses, drivers, routes, riders. The driver app does the road work. A stale location is not shown as live.

Parent representatives usually ask this first. It is built in. It is not a separate tracking company.

---

## 19. Finance

Collections you can defend in a board meeting.

Fee structures and payments in birr. Outstanding versus collected, by day and month. Parents see their own children’s balance. Finance staff manage. Directors oversee. Not a private cashier notebook.

---

## 20. HR — payroll

Payroll is built for Ethiopia — for every employee. Teachers, other staff, and drivers sit on one payroll.

The calculator uses **Proclamation 1395/2025**: monthly income tax, employee pension **7%** of basic salary, employer pension **11%**. You can type a salary and see the slip before you save.

Rates can change. HR should confirm against current law. Teachers do not see another person’s pay.

---

## 21. Store and inventory

The store is a command desk: items, purchase requests, issues, assets, suppliers.

This is where leakage usually hides in a paper school. Open purchase requests, issue requests, stock value, assets in repair — on one screen.

---

## 22. Inventory — stock trail

Every textbook and marker has a quantity and a movement.

On-hand versus reorder level. Stock in from a supplier. Stock out to an office. Books loaned to a student against a name and class.

---

## 23. Inventory — purchase and issue

Ask. Approve. Receive or issue. Same numbers for procurement, store keeper, and accountant.

No verbal “bring more chalk” without a request. Reports export Excel, CSV, or PDF.

---

## 24. School life

The school speaks in **one place**. Not four WhatsApp groups.

Announcements — directors post; people see by role. Gallery — events stay in the school, not on someone’s phone. Calendar — exams, holidays, meetings, sports day. Internal messages — teacher to parent, staff to staff, logged.

Amharic: **ማስታወቂያ፣ ጋለሪ፣ የቀን መቁጠሪያ እና መልእክት በአንድ ቦታ።**

---

## 25. Quality assurance

QA, this is your book. Findings live next to the school, not in a drawer.

Log a finding: classroom, library, transport, finance, student affairs. Open, in progress, closed. Severity. Leadership sees overdue follow-ups on the morning dashboard.

QA records. The duty owner executes. Directors do not hunt a paper file.

Amharic: **የጥራት ማረጋገጫ ግኝትና የማሻሻያ እቅድ በስርዓት።**

---

## 26. Quality assurance — plans

A finding is not finished until the plan has evidence.

Example: freeze grade publish until makeup marks are in. Owner: Examinations. Follow-up: section director. Parents never receive an unapproved mark while that plan is open. Transport, store, and student-affairs findings use the **same** book.

## 27. Settings and language

Each person chooses English, Amharic, or Afaan Oromo. It does not force the whole school.

Dark mode. Tile order — drag the home screen to match your duty. From Settings, directors can also download a **school JSON backup**.

## 28. Where files live

People will ask: cloud or our own computer?

**Recommended: both.**

Daily live work is in the cloud — students, grades, fees, store, buses, messages. Once a week, download a JSON copy onto a school computer or school server. Extra snapshot after payroll, exams, or a large enrollment.

A full on-site ERP server is optional extra work. A school-held copy is **not** optional. Passwords are not packed in that JSON.

## 30. How a week looks

Not a theory. A working week.

Day 1: office logs in. Rosters and passwords work.  
Day 2: teachers take attendance, post homework, enter grades.  
Day 3: section director approves. Student affairs logs a case if needed. QA opens a finding if needed.  
Day 4: parents link with ID and date of birth. They see homework, gallery, calendar, messages.  
Day 5: leadership prints a PDF. Store, payroll, or transport if you use them. Download a school JSON backup to a school PC.

Pilot: **30 to 60 days**, one campus or one grade band, then a board decision to expand.
