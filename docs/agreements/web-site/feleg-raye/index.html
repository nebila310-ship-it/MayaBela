Within the Contract Price, Provider includes a Strong Customization Package designed so Feleg Raye School is not forced onto a rigid one-size-fits-all setup:

  1. School branding: school name, logo placement in System surfaces where supported, color accents where applicable.
  2. Organizational mapping: campus/section structure, academic levels, class/section naming conventions.
  3. Role & workflow fit: mapping Feleg Raye titles to System roles; approval paths for grades, transfers, procurement/stores.
  4. Fee & operations configuration: fee categories/terms as agreed; transport routes/buses setup support.
  5. Report & form preferences: reasonable adaptation of key lists/exports and operational screens based on discovery workshop outputs.
  6. Included customization effort: up to 120 (one hundred twenty) professional service hours for configuration, workflow adaptation, and Feleg-Raye-specific adjustments during implementation.
  7. Priority change handling: during implementation, School-requested changes inside Scope are scheduled in weekly sprints; Provider shall not unreasonably refuse feasible in-platform customizations covered by remaining hours.

Work beyond the included hours, or net-new modules not listed in Clauses 4–5, shall be estimated via a written Change Order (scope, days, fee) and performed only after written approval by both Parties.
