1. Purpose and scope

1.1 Purpose

MaJo e-School Bridge is a Flutter web and mobile ERP for Ethiopian schools. One deployment serves many school tenants. Each school’s students, staff, marks, fees, and files must stay inside that school. This document is the requirements baseline for INSA asset registration: what the system is for, who may use it, which functions are critical, and which security controls are required.

1.2 In scope

  • School information: students, classes, attendance, homework, grades, admissions, parent links.
  • Operations: fees, inventory/procurement, HR/staff accounts, transport (buses, drivers, passenger lists, live position of the assigned bus).
  • Sensitive collections: health, counselling, safeguarding — restricted staff only.
  • Identity: school login, JWT school and role claims, optional/required MFA by role.
  • Platform owner console (provision schools; not a classroom role).
  • Hosted persistence (Postgres app_documents, storage buckets, Edge Functions) and dedicated/on-prem instances for schools that cannot share the hosted tenancy.

1.3 Out of scope

  • A school’s own campus CCTV, IoT, or blockchain — not objects of this product.
  • National PKI, INSA-approved proprietary crypto, or customer-managed disk keys on the current hosted database (operator residual; see CSMS-SUP-001).
  • Forcing MFA on classroom teachers, parents, students, or drivers (would lock live lessons).

2. Intended system behaviour

The system shall behave as a multi-tenant school ERP with need-to-know access:

  1. Tenant isolation. Every persistent row carries school_id. A JWT from School A cannot read or write School B. Isolation is enforced in the database (row-level security), not only in the client.
  2. Role-based desks. After login, the user sees modules for their role (teacher classroom, registrar, finance, driver, parent, student, platform owner). The client must not be the only gate: denied writes return an error and must not dump the whole school.
  3. Classroom continuity. Ordinary teachers write attendance, homework, and class notices for their assigned classes. They shall not receive payroll, HR, or whole-school children’s files they do not manage.
  4. Finance integrity. Recording that a fee is paid is a finance/cashier function. Homeroom shall not silently mark paid.
  5. Admissions. Creating or changing admission applications through the privileged registry path is a registrar (or equivalent) function, not every staff JWT.
  6. Transport need-to-know. A driver sees students whose transportId matches that driver’s busId, and may publish GPS/scans for that bus only.
  7. Public surface. The login page has no JWT. Only school logos (branding bucket) are public. Student files stay under schools/<school_id>/.
  8. Availability of teaching. Security changes that could stop a live school are applied on staging, then in a notified production window.

3. User roles

Identity is a username + role + school. The JWT stores school and role in app_metadata only (not in user-editable metadata). Staff desks use the templates in StaffRoles (a school may add a titled copy of a template; permissions stay those of the source template unless the school owner changes them in Role Settings).

7. Assumptions, dependencies, residuals

  • Each customer school is the controller of its children’s data; MaJo Bridge is the processor for the ERP.
  • Hosted Postgres, Auth, Storage, and Edge Functions are a sub-processor (see CSMS-SUP-001). Disk encryption keys on that host are the supplier’s — operator residual against CMCSRS §6.29.f.
  • Governmental / key-private locally licensed cloud (§6.29.k) is not claimed for the shared hosted project. Dedicated/on-prem is the path for schools that cannot share it.
  • This SRS does not replace INSA accreditation or an annual compliance letter.

8. Verification

Requirements FR-01–FR-09 and SR-01–SR-12 are verified in source by CAAT and CI (local Postgres role tests, driver 9/9, two-school 7/7, Flutter privileged-MFA tests, Deno Edge inventory). Live production is not mutated by this document.
