1. How people sign in

  1. Open the school URL. The login page shows that school’s logo only (no student names).
  2. Enter School ID, username, password, and role (Admin, Teacher, Parent, Student, or Driver). The same person may have more than one role — they pick the desk they are opening.
  3. First login: change a temporary password (10+ characters). Do not reuse the SMS/WhatsApp temp password.
  4. School Admin and Finance: after password, open Settings and enroll an authenticator. Login still works with the password; the system prompts until MFA is on. Teachers, principals, parents, students, and drivers are not forced to enroll (so a teacher without a phone is not locked out of class).

Eight failed tries in 15 minutes for the same username+role+school lock that combination briefly. That is not a school-wide outage.

3. Files, logos, and Maya Assistant

  • Do: put student photos and documents in the school file area (private). Put only the logo / stamp in branding (that is what the login page can show without a password).
  • Do not: upload a class photo, ID scan, or report card as the “logo”.
  • Maya Assistant (optional): it sees counts (how many students), not names. Do not type a child’s name, medical note, or password into the prompt. If the school forbids external AI, MaJo leaves the AI key unset — the rest of the ERP still works.

4. Passwords, shared computers, and leaving

  • Each person has their own username. A staff-room PC is common; sign out when you leave the desk. A stolen teacher session lasts until the login expires — teachers are not forced onto MFA.
  • Do not write passwords on the classroom board or in a staff WhatsApp group.
  • When someone leaves the school, Admin/HR disables that account the same day. Do not wait for “end of term”.
  • MaJo support: send counts and function names, never a spreadsheet of children, never the service-role key.

6. What to do when something looks wrong

  1. Do not delete evidence. Sign out if you think someone else used your desk.
  2. Tell the school admin. If children’s data may have left the school (public logo, WhatsApp class list, leaked password), they tell MaJo: +251 911 646 444 / nabilmaya6464@gmail.com.
  3. MaJo CSO follows CSMS-IRP-001 (contain, preserve, Ethio-CER2T if confirmed). Prefer disabling one account over taking the whole school offline.
