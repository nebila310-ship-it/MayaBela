3. Rules of engagement

  • Do: test tenant isolation (School A vs School B), teacher vs finance vs driver, login rate-limit, public branding vs private files, MFA prompt on Admin/Finance.
  • Do: use the synthetic school only. Eight failed logins in 15 minutes lock that username+role+school (not the whole site).
  • Do not: load-test or brute-force the live URL during school hours (09:00–18:00 East Africa Time) in a way that could stop attendance.
  • Do not: upload a class photo as the login logo; that bucket is public.
  • Do not: paste real student rows, medical notes, or passwords into Maya Assistant or into Hibir tickets.
  • Prefer disabling one test account over taking the school offline. Report findings to the CSO (CSMS-IRP-001).
