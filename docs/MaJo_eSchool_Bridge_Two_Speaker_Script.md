# MaJo e-School Bridge — 2-Speaker Script

**File:** `docs/MaJo_eSchool_Bridge_Two_Speaker_Presentation.pptx`  
**Presenter 1:** CEO Artist Desalegn Hailu (vision, problem, value)  
**Presenter 2:** Deputy Manager (features, ops, benefits)

---

## CEO — Slides 1–5

### Slide 1 — Opening
“Good morning/afternoon everyone. My name is Artist Desalegn Hailu, CEO of MaJo-Bridge Technology PLC.  
Today, we are pleased to introduce **MaJo e-School Bridge** — connecting schools, teachers, students, parents, and **transport handlers** in one system, with clear roles for Section Directors, Student Affairs, Quality Assurance, Finance, and HR.”

### Slide 2 — The Problem
Cover: paperwork · slow parent communication · scattered student info · hard performance tracking · transport/attendance gaps.  
Close: “How can technology make school management easier, smarter, and more connected?”

### Slide 3 — Our Solution
“Our answer is MaJo e-School Bridge.”  
**School · Teachers · Students · Parents · Transport** (five connections)  
Also flag leadership roles: Section Directors, Student Affairs, QA, Finance, HR.

### Slide 4 — Vision
“Our vision is to help transform traditional schools into smart, connected, and data-driven educational institutions.”

### Slide 5 — Why MaJo? + handoff
One platform · web & mobile · real-time · communication · efficiency · scalable.  
Then: “Now, I would like to invite our Deputy Manager to explain how MaJo works and the key services it provides.”

---

## Deputy Manager — Slides 6–14

### Slide 6 — How MaJo Works (5 users)
Admin · Teachers · Students · Parents · **Transport Handler**

### Slide 7 — Leadership & Support Roles (NEW — emphasize)
- **Section Directors** — classes, teacher assign, grade approval  
- **Student Affairs** — welfare, transfers, student services  
- **Quality Assurance** — standards & academic quality  
- **Finance** — fees & financial visibility  
- **Human Resources** — staff/teacher accounts & access  

### Slide 8 — School Management
Registration, SIS, attendance, exams/grades, timetable, calendar, reports, staff/HR.  
Remind: leadership roles share one dashboard with controlled permissions.

### Slide 9 — Teacher & Student Services
Teachers: attendance, assignments, quizzes, grades, tracking, parent comms.  
Students: resources, assignments, quizzes, results, announcements.

### Slide 10 — Parent Application
Attendance · performance · announcements · teacher chat · fees · transport.  
“Instead of waiting for a report or visiting the school, parents can stay informed directly through the platform.”

### Slide 11 — Transport Handler Portal
School → **Transport Handler** → Student → Parent  
Driver app · live tracking · routes · pickup/drop-off · parent alerts.

### Slide 12 — AI-Powered Future
AI Tutor · AI for Teachers · AI for Parents · AI Academic Analytics.

### Slide 13 — Benefits for Schools
Paperwork ↓ · time ↓ · communication ↑ · transparency ↑ · performance monitoring · parent engagement · data-driven decisions.

### Slide 14 — Partnership Opportunity
Digital transformation · new services · competitiveness · parent engagement · scalable tech · long-term records.  
Then invite CEO back.

---

## CEO Returns — Slides 15–17

### Slide 15 — Why Partner
“We believe the future of education is connected, digital, and intelligent…”  
**Technology + Education + Partnership = Better Schools**

### Slide 16 — Roadmap
Phase 1 School Management → 2 Parent/Teacher/Transport services → 3 Digital Learning → 4 AI → 5 Regional Expansion.

### Slide 17 — Closing
Bridge between schools, teachers, students, parents, **and transport handlers** — with Section Directors, Student Affairs, QA, Finance, and HR as one team.  
Invite partners. Thank you.
