1. What this paper does

This is one financial proposal made from two source papers: the TOR V3 academic list and MaJo e-School Bridge Financial Proposal 2. TOR modules 5.1–5.21 are priced by difficulty (not one average). Those 21 lines add to 2,430,000 ETB. Customization and go-live is 500,000 ETB, so the TOR choice is 2,930,000 ETB before VAT.

The balance from Financial Proposal 2’s complete-platform total of 4,620,000 ETB is 1,690,000 ETB. That balance is allocated across the leftover modules (the desks that sit outside TOR 5.1–5.21). Add them to the TOR pack and the complete platform is again 4,620,000 ETB before VAT: 2,430,000 + 1,690,000 + 500,000.

The 500,000 ETB customization and go-live fee applies in both calculations. It is not added twice if LIA later moves from Option 1 to Option 2 — only the leftover-module line of 1,690,000 is added.
