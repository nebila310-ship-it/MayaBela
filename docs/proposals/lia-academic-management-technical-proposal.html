2. Executive summary

Lebawi International Academy asked for one Academic Management System that replaces Excel grading, paper summaries, and disconnected tools. Majo Bridge Technologies and Events has implemented that system as MaJo e-School Bridge.

In everyday words, LIA now has:

  • A single student record (SIS) that teachers, directors, counselors, and parents share — with the right limits.
  • A learning space (LMS) built from lesson plans, homework, materials, exams, and class discussion — not a second school.
  • A markbook that is the gradebook. Exams can push into it. Parents only see marks after approve-and-publish.
  • Attendance that feeds at-risk flags and analytics. A student is flagged when low marks and high absence occur together.
  • Student support, health, discipline, safeguarding, DoSA programs, QA, and leadership desks — each on its own official register.
  • Reports that export the same live data as Excel, CSV, or PDF, so offices stop keeping a second spreadsheet.
  • Role-based access, audit logs, parent/student/teacher portals, and a go-live desk for privacy, MFA, snapshots, and training.

The TOR sections on reporting, roles, integration, privacy, performance, implementation, deliverables, and success criteria are implemented. The remaining work is school operation: data, training, and the go-live checklist — not a second product to build.

4. What LIA asked for

The Terms of Reference asked for a modern Academic Management System that covers the student journey and the offices around it. In short:

  • Replace manual Excel grading and paper-based summaries.
  • Connect SIS, LMS, assessment, gradebook, attendance, analytics, and communication.
  • Serve IT Admin, Academic Directors (DoES / DoMS / DoHS, DoSA), teachers, students, parents, leadership, counselors, career guidance, QA, and department heads.
  • Protect student data with roles, audit, encryption, MFA, and backups.
  • Report performance, grades, attendance trends, teacher insight, and at-risk students in real time.
  • Engage parents on the portal or mobile app.

The following sections show that this is not a plan. It is the running product, mapped to those words.

8. User roles and access control — how it feels at LIA

A person signs in with School ID + username/phone + password. The system then shows only that person’s desks.

  • A Grade 8 teacher sees her classes, takes attendance, enters marks, and messages the linked parent. She does not open safeguarding.
  • The DoMS Section Director approves grades, reviews lesson plans, opens reports, and records academic evaluations.
  • DoSA works clubs, grievances, counseling, and internships. Grievances stay on the DoSA register; they are not mixed into discipline.
  • QA records observations and opens the audit log. QA scores do not overwrite curriculum evaluations.
  • A parent sees only linked children, and only published grades.
  • IT / Administration Staff run Friday snapshots, MFA help, and device lists without receiving Full Access.

The school owner (or Full Access) is the only grantor of the highest role. That matches a board-controlled school.

9. Integrations — one school, not four products

Offices often fear “integration” means buying connectors. Here it means the desks already share the same child.

  • Create a student once in SIS. LMS, attendance, markbook, fees, bus, and support all use that id.
  • Give an exam. The score can enter the markbook. Analytics reads the markbook. Parents see it only after publish.
  • Mark a child absent. The parent can be notified. Attendance intelligence updates. DoSA oversight reads the same sessions.
  • Message a parent. Teacher and parent share one thread. Class discussion is a named group on the same message store.

10. Data privacy and security — in school language

  • Least privilege. If your role does not include a desk, the sidebar hides it.
  • School isolation. LIA data is not visible to another school on the same platform.
  • Consent and rights. Parents and students can record consent and file access or erasure requests. Staff process the queue on Go-live.
  • Secrets. Snapshots do not copy passwords or authenticator secrets.
  • MFA. Leadership and finance can enrol an authenticator. It is available now; the school chooses who must use it.
  • Backup rhythm. Administration Staff write a school snapshot from Go-live. The desk reminds them after 24 hours. Platform-owner restore remains a separate drill.

11. Non-functional requirements — honest wording

LIA asked for one thousand concurrent users, 99.5% uptime, an intuitive UI, and modular growth. MaJo e-School Bridge is built as a multi-school cloud system with those numbers as the go-live target. The go-live desk itself tells staff: health signals are recorded; the targets are not pretended to be a finished laboratory certificate.

Usability is already in the product: short student ids, role doors, English / Amharic / Oromo, and training articles. Expansion is modular: LIA can switch on transport, inventory, or CCTV without rebuilding SIS.

14. Day-to-day use at Lebawi International Academy

Morning

Homeroom takes attendance. Absent and late parents can be notified. DoSA can see attendance oversight without a second register.

Teaching

The teacher opens LMS for that class + subject: today’s plan, homework, materials, or an exam. Discussion stays in the class thread. The SIS roster is one tap away.

Marking

Scores go into the weighted markbook. An exam attempt can be pushed there. The Section Director approves. Only then do parents see the mark.

Care

Counseling, IEP, and college guidance stay on Student support. Health stays on health records. Safeguarding stays sealed.

Friday

Administration Staff open Go-live, write the school snapshot if the 24-hour reminder is on, and walk the short digital-ops checklist. MaJo support joins only if the whole school cannot log in.
