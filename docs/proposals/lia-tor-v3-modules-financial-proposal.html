1. Purpose

Lebawi International Academy asked for a financial proposal on Terms of Reference V3 for an Academic Operations Management System. This paper prices that TOR, not a product catalogue. Schedule A copies the TOR module titles from sections 5.1 to 5.21. Each module is marked Implemented. Each module is charged at the same average unit price so the licence pack stays at 2,930,000 ETB before VAT. Subscription and annual support remain a separate proposal.

2. Average pricing

21 TOR core modules × average 139,524 ETB = 2,930,004 ETB. The commercial pack is capped at 2,930,000 ETB VAT exclusive (4 ETB rounding absorbed by the supplier).

  • One average unit price on every TOR module — 139,524 ETB, VAT exclusive.
  • Pack total before VAT — 2,930,000 ETB (does not exceed the cap).
  • VAT 15% — 439,500 ETB.
  • Total payable — 3,369,500 ETB.

3. Payment

  • 40% on signing — 1,347,800 ETB including VAT.
  • 40% on delivery for user-acceptance review — 1,347,800 ETB including VAT.
  • 20% on go-live — 673,900 ETB including VAT.
