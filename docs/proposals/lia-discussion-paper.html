4. Five sentences that cover the TOR

If the room only remembers five lines, use these.

  1. One child. SIS is the student. LMS, attendance, markbook, fees, bus, and support all use that id.
  2. One gradebook. Teachers enter marks in the weighted markbook. Exams can push into it. Parents see marks only after approve-and-publish.
  3. One attendance story. The daily register feeds trends, parent notices, DoSA oversight, and at-risk flags.
  4. One conversation. Parent–teacher messages and class discussion live in the communication platform. Not a Telegram group as the official record.
  5. One door per person. Roles hide desks. Safeguarding never appears to parents, students, or classroom teachers.

5. Discussion by TOR area

Each block is a prompt. Read the question, let LIA talk, then point at the desk. Do not debate whether the module exists. Debate how LIA will run it.

5.1 Replacing Excel and paper summaries

Question for the room: which office still keeps a private grading spreadsheet today, and who will stop treating it as the master after go-live?

  • Live answer: Markbook is the master. Reports → Academic / Grade analytics workbook downloads a copy.
  • LIA action: Name the Section Director who approves marks before parents see them.

5.2 Student performance, grades, attendance, at-risk

Question: when a Grade 8 child is slipping, who at LIA should see the name first — homeroom, DoMS, counselor, or QA?

  • Live answer: At-risk & insights flags the child only when average is below 50% and absence is high enough. Analytics and QA snapshots read the same stores.
  • LIA action: Agree the follow-up path. The software flags. People still counsel.
If someone asks for “predictive AI,” say: “We implemented the TOR need — finding at-risk students — with a rule you can audit. We did not hide a model you cannot explain to a parent.”

5.3 LMS, assessment, and the gradebook

Question: should LIA keep a separate learning app, or is class + subject on the LMS hub enough for this year?

  • Live answer: LMS hub is lesson plans, homework, materials, exams, class discussion, and the SIS roster. Exam attempts can push into the markbook.
  • LIA action: Pick one class for the first live week so teachers are not asked to move every subject on day one.

5.4 Parents and the mobile app

Question: who at LIA will approve parent-link requests in the first two weeks — homeroom, registrar, or Student Affairs?

  • Live answer: Parent signs in with School ID and the phone on file, links with student id + date of birth, then waits for approval. They then see published grades, attendance, homework, fees, bus, messages.
  • LIA action: Write that approver’s name in Section 8.

5.5 DoSA, counseling, career guidance, safeguarding

Question: is LIA comfortable that counselors and career guidance sit on the Student Affairs role, and that safeguarding is a sealed desk?

  • Live answer: We did not invent extra staff-role keys. DoSA, counseling, and college guidance use Student Affairs. Safeguarding cases never go to parents, students, or classroom teachers. Grievances stay on DoSA; they are not mixed into discipline.
  • LIA action: Name DoSA and the safeguarding officer. Confirm they accept this mapping.

5.6 Academic Directors and department heads

Question: will DoES, DoMS, and DoHS each hold a Section Director login, and will department heads share that academic-lead desk?

  • Live answer: Section Director is the Academic Director / department-head mapping: classes, students, exam approval, reports, curriculum review, academic evaluations.
  • LIA action: One named director per school (elementary / middle / high), plus any department head who must review plans.

5.7 QA, leadership, audit

Question: should QA be read-only on academics and write-only on observations, audits, and the audit log?

  • Live answer: QA observations are not teacher_evaluations. Curriculum evaluations stay on the curriculum desk. QA and Vice Principal can open the audit log. Leadership meetings sit on the staff calendar.
  • LIA action: Name the QA lead and whether the Vice Principal also watches the log.

5.8 IT, privacy, MFA, daily snapshot

Question: who is LIA’s Administration Staff (IT Admin) for Friday snapshots and MFA help — without giving them Full Access?

  • Live answer: IT Admin maps to Administration Staff: digital ops, go-live, CCTV, system health. MFA is an authenticator people can enrol. Go-live reminds the school when the last snapshot is older than 24 hours. That is a checklist, not a second backup product.
  • LIA action: Name the Friday owner. Decide who must enrol MFA (recommend: finance, registrar, owner).
