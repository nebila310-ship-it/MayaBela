1. Purpose of this inception report

Lebawi International Academy has awarded Majo Bridge Technologies and Events the contract to implement its Academic Management System on MaJo e-School Bridge. LIA has asked for an inception report. This document answers that request. It has five jobs:

  • Restate what LIA asked for in the TOR and confirm that our understanding is still correct after the award.
  • Fix the scope: the modules, portals, and services inside the contract, and the items outside it.
  • Lay out the method, the week-by-week work plan, the deliverables, and the acceptance test for each one.
  • Name the people on both sides, the meetings, and the way decisions and changes are recorded.
  • List the risks, assumptions, and inputs LIA must provide, so nothing is discovered late.

The software itself is already built and live. The TOR mapping in the Technical Proposal stands. This report is therefore about the school cutover: configuring LIA, loading its data, training its people, testing with its officers, and going live — not about building a new product.

3. Our understanding of the assignment

LIA wants one system that replaces Excel grading, paper summaries, and informal messaging groups, and that gives every office the same student record. The TOR success criteria (§12) are the yardstick for this project:

TOR success criterionHow LIA will see it at go-live
Elimination of Excel-based gradingTeachers enter marks in the Weighted Markbook. Section Directors approve. Excel exists only as an export.
Real-time academic reportingDashboards, analytics, and Reports read today's marks and attendance. No office keeps a second spreadsheet as the master.
Full parent engagement via portal or mobile appApproved parents see published grades, attendance, homework, and messages for their own children on web or the Android app.
Improved academic performance trackingAt-risk flags (low marks + high absence), SIS history, and report cards are produced by rule from live data.
Reduced administrative workloadOne register, one approve-and-publish path, one message thread per family, one export button.

Serving offices: IT Admin, Academic Directors (DoES / DoMS / DoHS), DoSA, teachers, students, parents, leadership, counselors and career guidance, QA, and department heads — each with role-based access, audit, and school-isolated data.

12. Hosting, security, and data protection

12.1 Default: Majo Bridge cloud

  • Web app on the live URL with HTTPS; data in the hosted database with encryption at rest; row-level security so a teacher sees assigned classes and a parent sees linked children; LIA data isolated from every other school.
  • MFA (authenticator app) available for leadership, finance, and IT; the school decides who must enrol.
  • School snapshots from Go-live by LIA IT (weekly, reminder after 24 hours), plus platform backups.
  • Audit logs: school audit log, platform audit log, grade audit log. Open to leadership and QA.
  • Privacy desk: consent register, subject-access export, and erasure that redacts personal fields while keeping the academic record intact. Safeguarding and counseling notes are never in parent or student exports.

12.2 Option: hybrid hosting (Addendum A)

If LIA elects hybrid hosting at the inception meeting, sensitive records (SIS identity, marks, health, safeguarding, discipline, counseling) have their master copy on an LIA server; the cloud carries the app, login routing, published parent views, and public-internet services. LIA then provides, before W3: a server (or pair) in a locked room, UPS, campus LAN reaching staff rooms, a static internal address, and a named IT owner. Majo Bridge provides the installation, the sync rules, and the restore drill. The hybrid readiness line is added to Section 6 and the go-live date is re-confirmed.

12.3 Data-protection commitments during the project

  • Majo Bridge staff access LIA data only for configuration, import, training, and support, and only through named accounts that appear in the audit log.
  • No student data is used for demonstrations to other schools. Test data in UAT is LIA's own data under LIA's control.
  • Templates and exports are exchanged through the project channel agreed at inception, not public links.
