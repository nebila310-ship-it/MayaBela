## Bunna International Bank S.C. — combined technical and financial partnership proposal

Prepared by **Majo Bridge Technologies and Events PLC** for **Majo Bridge OS**. Cream-paper PDF and Word. Every page carries document number `MBT-BUNNA-PROP-2026-01` and page X of Y. Fees differ by region. Addis Ababa: ETB 1,250,000 configuration per school (excl. VAT) plus 3.5% of student payments received through the Bank (excl. VAT). Other regions: ETB 800,000 configuration per school (excl. VAT) plus 3.2% of student fees collected (excl. VAT). Salary and staff payments are excluded. Hosting is covered by the technology provider. Training outside Addis Ababa: the Bank covers accommodation and transport for 2–3 Provider staff. Annex B is the commercial proforma.
