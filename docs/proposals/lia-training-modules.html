Training Modules and Trainer's Guide — Student Management System (SMS) Project at Lebawi International Academy

Prepared for Lebawi International Academy (LIA), Addis Ababa

Document MBT-LIA-TRN-2026-01 · 27 October 2026 · Version 1.0
Companion to the Inception Report MBT-LIA-INC-2026-01, Section 11 (Training plan) and Annex C (Training timetable). Deliverable D6 — Training delivered.
What it contains One module per training session in Annex C: who attends, what the trainer teaches, in which order and for how many minutes, the exercise each attendee completes on real LIA data, and how learning is checked before the session is signed off.
Prepared for Principal (owner of D6), IT Admin, Section Directors (DoES / DoMS / DoHS), DoSA, QA officer, Registrar, Department Heads, teachers, and the LIA focal person.
How to use it The trainer follows the minute plan. The LIA focal person books the room and the attendees, prints the attendance sheet (Annex T-1), and files the competency checklist (Annex T-3) after each session.
Training method. Every session uses the same three steps: show (the trainer demonstrates the task on the projector), do (each attendee repeats the task on their own login, on real LIA data, in the live system), and check (the trainer confirms the result on screen and ticks the competency checklist). No session is a lecture. No desk is opened to a user before that user has done the task once in training.

Before every session (LIA focal person and trainer)

  • Room from Annex T-4: projector, Wi-Fi or LAN, one device per attendee (laptop or tablet; teachers may use their phone for the Android part).
  • Every attendee already has a login created in T1/T2 and has received the School ID and a temporary password on paper.
  • Real LIA data is loaded: the roster signed at M4 (Fri 20 Nov) for T4 onward; the pilot section's classes for teacher cohorts.
  • Attendance sheet (Annex T-1) and competency checklist (Annex T-3) printed; one trainer, one helper for groups above twelve.

Rules that apply in every session

  • Attendees sign in with their own account, never the trainer's.
  • Anything typed in training is real: use the practice class "Training 0A" for grades and messages, and real records only for look-ups until the trainer says otherwise.
  • Passwords are changed on first login during the session; nobody leaves on a temporary password.
  • Questions outside the module go on the car-park sheet and are answered in the clinic hour or by the issue log.
  • Session closes with the evaluation form (Annex T-2) and the trainer's tick on the checklist.
