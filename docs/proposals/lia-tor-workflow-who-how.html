2. The school year in one line

TOR 5.1 asked for one student lifecycle. That is the spine. Every module below hangs on it.

1. Inquiry 2. Application 3. Admission exam 4. Offer / waitlist 5. Enrollment 6. Teach · attend · assess · support 7. Promote / transfer 8. Graduate 9. Alumni

One child, one id. Admissions writes the first card. SIS holds it. LMS, markbook, attendance, DoSA, counseling, and the parent door all use that id. Excel is an export, not the master.

3. A normal LIA day

4. Each TOR module — who uses it, how, and how LIA customizes it

Numbers match TOR V3 Section 5. The live product name is in italics.

TOR 5.1

Student Information System (SIS) — Students, Add Student, Transfers & Promotion, Alumni

Who: Registrar (master). Section Directors and DoSA (oversight). Teachers (own class list). Counselors (profile they are allowed to see). Parents/students (own published view only).

How: Registrar creates the child (one record or Excel/CSV). Profile holds class, house, medical flag, parents, documents. Promotion, transfer, and withdrawal run as an approval path LIA names. After graduation the same id becomes alumni — not a side spreadsheet.

Customize: Grade names, houses, campuses (KG / Primary / Middle / High), required documents, who may promote a year.

TOR 5.2

Admissions Management — Admissions

Who: Admissions / Registrar. Academic directors (exam score / offer). Leadership (analytics).

How: Family applies. Staff move the file through LIA stages (inquiry → application → exam → waitlist → offer → enrol). Documents are checked on the file. Entrance exam score sits on the same card. When enrolled, the child becomes an SIS student — not a second list.

Customize: Application stages, exam paper, waitlist rule, who may issue an offer, what analytics the admissions board sees.

TOR 5.3

Academic Management — Academic Management, Timetable, Classroom Teachers

Who: DoES / DoMS / DoHS, Department Heads, Registrar. Teachers read their timetable. Students/parents see the published week.

How: Leadership sets the year, terms, classes, and subjects. Teachers are allocated to class + subject. The timetable is the weekly grid. Conflicts are caught when two things occupy the same slot.

Customize: LIA’s three (or more) school levels, term dates, period lengths, how homeroom vs subject teachers are named.

TOR 5.4

Learning Management System (LMS) — LMS, Homework, Lesson plans, e-Books & Materials

Who: Subject teacher (writes). Students (receive and submit). Parents (see posted work). DH / Section Director (review plans).

How: Teacher opens the hub for that class + subject: plan, notes, worksheets, exams, live or recorded class link, discussion. Students submit homework there. This is the replacement for Telegram teaching and scattered files.

Customize: What a student may open, whether a plan must be approved before class, file types, who hosts the live-class link.

TOR 5.5

Curriculum Management — Curriculum office, Lesson plans

Who: Department Heads, curriculum lead, QA (read alignment), teachers (teach the unit).

How: Units live on the curriculum desk with version history. Lesson plans attach to those units. DH reviews. Feedback from teachers, parents, or QA can sit on the same office — not a side Word file.

Customize: National vs international map LIA uses, who must sign a unit change, how often plans are reviewed.

TOR 5.6

Assessment & Examination — Exam bank & papers, Examinations & Grade Approvals

Who: Teachers (set and mark). DH / Section Directors (approve). Exam office (schedule, templates, archive). Students (sit the paper they are given).

How: Teacher or exam office schedules a paper (online or in class). Question bank and templates hold LIA’s standard and national/model papers. Attempts can push a score into the markbook. Historical papers stay in the bank.

Customize: Exam calendar, template, who may open the bank, secure handling rules (who prints, who sees before the sitting).

TOR 5.7

Grading & Markbook — Weighted Markbook, Grade Workflow, Report Cards, Analytics

Who: Teacher (enters). DH / Section Director (moderates and approves). Leadership (locks / reports). Parent and student (published card only).

How: This is the Excel replacement. Homework, quiz, assignment, exam sit as components with LIA weights. The system calculates. DH reviews. Grade is locked with an audit trail. Report card is printed from the same book. Trends by subject and assessment type sit on analytics — not a second sheet.

Customize: Category weights, continuous vs term vs final, scale (letters or numbers), who must approve before parents see a mark, report-card layout.

TOR 5.8

Attendance — Attendance, At-risk & insights

Who: Homeroom / subject teacher (takes). DoSA and Section Directors (oversight). Parents (notified). Analytics (patterns).

How: Daily register. Late and absent can notify the linked parent. Absence patterns feed at-risk (with marks). DoSA does not keep a second paper list.

Customize: Homeroom vs period attendance, how many minutes is “late,” who receives the SMS/app notice, the at-risk rule (for example low marks + high absence).

TOR 5.9

Behavior & Discipline — Student Affairs & Discipline

Who: DoSA / discipline officers. Teachers (log an incident they saw). Section Directors (escalation). Parents (notified when LIA says so). Not classroom teachers for the full file.

How: Incident is logged, linked to the student, escalated on LIA’s ladder, parent notified. Code-of-conduct categories are LIA’s words. Separate from safeguarding.

Customize: Incident types, who may escalate, when a parent is told, what a teacher may log vs what stays on DoSA only.

TOR 5.10

Student Health — Student support (health records)

Who: Clinic / nurse. Registrar (medical flag on SIS). Leadership (health report, not the full note). Parents (as LIA policy allows).

How: Clinic log, vaccinations, emergency note, medication. The SIS only shows a flag so a teacher knows to send the child to clinic — not the whole file. Inventory of clinic stock if LIA keeps it here (academic TOR; not the school store).

Customize: Who may open a health note, which allergies show on the class list, emergency contact order.

TOR 5.11

Counseling & Student Support — Student support, Safeguarding

Who: Counselors (sessions, appointments, SEL). Safeguarding officer (CP cases — sealed). Parents (secure message when the counselor opens it). Referrals to other offices.

How: Appointment, session note, case track. Child-protection files are hidden from parents, students, and classroom teachers. Referrals stay on the same student id.

Customize: Who is the named CP officer, retention of notes, which parent messages are allowed, SEL fields LIA wants to see.

TOR 5.12

College Guidance — Student support (college), Events, Calendar

Who: Career / college counselor. Upper-school students. Parents (events and published portfolio items). DoSA (internships overlap).

How: Direction → readiness → application → decision on the student’s file. Portfolio, appointments, college events on the calendar. Not a parallel Excel of universities.

Customize: Grade when college guidance starts, which documents sit in the portfolio, event types.

TOR 5.13

Special Needs & Inclusive Education — Student support (IEP)

Who: SEN / inclusion lead, assigned teachers, counselor. Parents (commitment agreement and reviews). Clinic (check-up forms if linked).

How: Intake assessment, IEP, parent agreement, incident/accident if related, evaluation. Training articles for teachers sit on Go-live / LMS — not a public student file.

Customize: IEP template, review cycle, which teachers see the IEP, parent-sign step.

TOR 5.14

Student Affairs (DoSA) — Student programs, Student Affairs, Attendance oversight

Who: DoSA. Scholarship desk. Internship/career. Transport coordinator (academic coordination only). Event leads.

How: Scholarships against live academic performance. Grievances on a DoSA register (not mixed into discipline unless LIA says so). Internships / Gojo hours. Attendance oversight of the same register teachers already take. Graduation and school events. Transport coordination uses the student list — this TOR does not include a full fleet ERP.

Customize: Scholarship rule, grievance steps, which clubs DoSA owns vs Section Directors, how internships are counted.

TOR 5.15

Parent Portal — Parents & Link Approvals, parent door, Messages, Calendar

Who: Linked guardians (after school approval). Teachers (message). Registrar (approve the link).

How: Parent links with student id + date of birth. School approves. Then: published grades, attendance, behavior the school released, teacher thread, events. Replaces Telegram groups, communication books, and printed reports as the daily channel. Draft marks never appear.

Customize: What a parent may see, who must approve a new link, language (English / Amharic / Afaan Oromo), whether behavior incidents go to the parent automatically.

TOR 5.16

Teacher Management (academic) — Lesson plans, Markbook, LMS, Classroom Teachers

Who: Classroom teachers. DH (review). HR only creates the login — this TOR is academic performance, not payroll.

How: Teacher submits plans, designs assessments, enters grades, runs the class hub. Academic tracking is the markbook and DH evaluation — not an HR appraisal form.

Customize: Plan deadline, whether a substitute may enter marks, which classes a teacher sees.

TOR 5.17

Department Head & Academic Leadership — Curriculum office, Examinations & Grade Approvals, lesson-plan review

Who: Department Heads, DoES / DoMS / DoHS, Principal / VP as LIA maps them.

How: Review lesson plans. Approve or return marks. Watch curriculum alignment. Record academic teacher evaluations. Meetings and minutes on the academic desk.

Customize: Who is DH for each department, two-step vs one-step grade approval, meeting types.

TOR 5.18

Leadership & Special Programs — Student programs, Calendar, Messages, Reports

Who: Leadership team, club sponsors, Gojo / extracurricular leads, DoSA.

How: Leadership meetings on the shared calendar. Secure group thread for the leadership set. Tasks and club registers. Participation and engagement reports that read SIS — not a side club Excel.

Customize: Which titles sit in the leadership chat, club list, how participation is scored, who signs a Gojo hour.

TOR 5.19

Quality Assurance — QA Findings & Plans, Audit Log, Analytics

Who: QA staff. Directors (overdue actions). Teachers (survey / observation they are assigned). DH evaluations stay on curriculum — QA does not overwrite them.

How: Academic audits, teaching observations, surveys, action research, student-performance analytics. Duty owner closes the finding. Audit log shows who changed a mark or a role.

Customize: Observation form, survey audience, who owns an action, when an item is “overdue.”

TOR 5.20

Communication & Collaboration — Messages, Announcements, Notifications, LMS discussion

Who: Teachers, parents, students (class thread), leadership (announcements), DoSA.

How: One parent–teacher thread (not four Telegram groups). School announcement to a named audience. Class discussion on the LMS hub. Notifications follow the event (absent, published grade, new homework).

Customize: Who may announce school-wide, whether students message teachers, quiet hours, which events trigger a parent notice.

TOR 5.21

Reporting & Analytics — Analytics & exports, At-risk & insights, Reports, Report Cards

Who: Section Directors, QA, DoSA, Principal. Teachers (own class insight). Board (exports). Parents (the published report card only).

How: Dashboards read live marks and attendance. At-risk is a clear rule (not a hidden score). Excel / CSV / PDF are copies of the live register — they replace manual Excel reporting and paper summaries. Predictive “at-risk” in TOR language is this rule plus trend, auditable by a director.

Customize: Which charts a role sees, the at-risk formula LIA will stand behind, report-card and export layouts from discovery.

5. Roles — who may open what (TOR 6)

A person signs in with School ID + phone/username + password. The sidebar then shows only that person’s desks. Granular ticks and the audit log are on Role Permissions and Audit Log.

TOR roleHow they typically use the system
IT Admin / Administration StaffLogins, MFA help, snapshots, devices. Not Full Access unless the owner grants it.
Academic Directors (DoES / DoMS / DoHS)Approvals, analytics, curriculum watch, their school’s teachers and classes.
DoSADiscipline, programs, grievances, attendance oversight, scholarships — not the whole markbook unless LIA adds it.
Department HeadsLesson-plan review, moderation of marks, department meetings.
TeachersOwn classes: attendance, LMS, markbook entry. No safeguarding file.
Counselors / Career guidanceSupport and college desks. CP only if named.
QA StaffFindings, surveys, audit log, analytics. Does not overwrite DH evaluations.
LeadershipDashboards, meetings, special programs, overdue QA.
StudentsOwn timetable, homework, materials, exams, announcements.
ParentsApproved children only; published grades only.

Customize: LIA maps real titles onto this catalogue. Extra ticks per person. The owner (or Full Access) is the only grantor of the highest role.

6. How the modules connect (TOR 7)

TOR integrationWhat staff actually see
LMS ↔ SISThe LMS roster is the SIS class list. Add a child once.
Assessment ↔ GradebookAn exam attempt can write the markbook. No parallel grade file.
Attendance ↔ AnalyticsToday’s register feeds at-risk and director dashboards.
Communication platformThe same student id is the parent thread, the announcement audience, and the class discussion.

Architecture (TOR 4): web + Android, role-based, modular. Cloud, hybrid, or campus-first placement is a hosting choice (see the hybrid addendum if LIA uses a school server for sensitive records). The workflow above does not change.

7. Implementation steps (TOR 10) — who does what

The product desks exist. These steps are LIA cutover, with customization at each phase.

TOR phaseWhoHow it is used / what happens
1. Planning & DesignMaJo-Bridge + LIA Directors, Registrar, DoSA, QA, ITValidate this TOR against the live desks. Write LIA’s workflow: weights, approval chain, houses, parent-visible items. Confirm cloud vs hybrid hosting.
2. Development / ConfigurationMaJo-Bridge (configure). LIA names the rules.Turn on the academic pack. Map roles. Set grade workflow, calendar, classes. Integrations in Section 6 are already the same student id — we confirm them on LIA data.
3. TestingTeachers, DH, one parent test account, QAFunctional pass on the live URL. UAT: take attendance, enter a mark, approve, confirm the test parent does not see the draft. Sign the UAT sheet.
4. DeploymentRegistrar (import). HR/academic (teacher logins). MaJo-Bridge (go-live).Student CSV/Excel into SIS. First parent-link approvals. Go-live on https://mayabela.pages.dev. Hard-refresh Ctrl+Shift+R after a release.
5. Training & SupportMaJo-Bridge + LIA office leadsTeachers, admin, parents. Manuals on Go-live → Training. Support Mon–Sat 09:00–18:00 EAT, +251 911 646 444 / +251 911 133 548. Full outage: call or WhatsApp.

Deliverables (TOR 11) in workflow language: the academic system; configured SIS/LMS/assessment desks; import tool; in-app manuals; scheduled training; this paper plus the technical proposal as the support map. Success (TOR 12): no private grading Excel as master; directors see today’s marks and absence; approved parents use portal or app; at-risk names appear from LIA’s rule; offices stop retyping the same child.

Customization during implementation. Kickoff workshop, role map, branding, class structure, grade weights, parent-link rules, and reasonable in-platform changes are part of going live. A net-new desk not in TOR V3 is a written change order and is built on the same student id.
