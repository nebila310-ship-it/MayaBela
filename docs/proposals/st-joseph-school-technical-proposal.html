2. Executive summary for management

St. Joseph School wants one digital school — not another spreadsheet, not a WhatsApp group, and not a tool that only the registrar can open. MaJo e-School Bridge is a school operating system already in production. It covers the offices that run a school: academics, students, parents, fees, store, staff, buses, quality, and ICT.

In management language, the school gets:

  • One student record that teachers, the registrar, student affairs, and parents share — with the right limits.
  • Marks that stay inside the school until leadership publishes them. Parents never see an unapproved grade.
  • Attendance, homework, timetable, exams, and report cards on the same child.
  • Fees, inventory, payroll, and transport as school offices, not as side files.
  • 61 modules that the school can switch on in bands (starter ≤20, standard 20–40, full 61). The financial proposal prices those bands per student.
  • Besrate Gabriel School in Dire Dawa as a second campus on the same product, with free installation as a social contribution (see the financial proposal).

The remaining work after contract is school operation: student lists, staff roles, training, and the go-live checklist — not a second product to invent.

4. What St. Joseph School receives

Five doors. One School ID. Nobody sees another school.

Office / ERP

Owner, principal, VP, registrar, student affairs, finance, HR, QA, ICT. Run the school from the 61-module sidebar.

Teacher

Homeroom and subject teachers. Attendance, homework, markbook, lesson plans, parent messages — own classes only.

Parent

Linked guardians only. Published grades, attendance, homework, fees, bus, messages. Never another family’s child.

Student

Own timetable, homework, materials, exams, announcements. The school sets from which grade a student may log in.

Driver

Assigned bus, passenger list, QR, live map. Parents see the same route from their own door.

8. A school day on the system

  1. Morning. Homeroom takes attendance. Parents of absent children can be notified. The dashboard shows the school.
  2. Lessons. Teachers follow the timetable, post homework, open e-books for that class only.
  3. Marks. Scores enter the weighted markbook. They stay unpublished until Examinations & Grade Approvals.
  4. Office. Registrar admits a child. Finance records a fee. Store issues a book. HR records leave.
  5. Afternoon bus. Driver opens the passenger list. Parent sees live GPS for the linked child.
  6. Evening. Parent opens published homework and messages the teacher. Leadership reviews QA or at-risk flags.

10. Data, privacy, and school isolation

  • Each campus has its own school ID. Announcements, e-books, fees, and report cards do not leak across schools.
  • HTTPS in transit. Hosted encryption at rest. Optional authenticator (MFA).
  • Safeguarding and counseling notes are excluded from parent/student exports.
  • The school can take a snapshot from Go-live. The school remains the owner of its records.
