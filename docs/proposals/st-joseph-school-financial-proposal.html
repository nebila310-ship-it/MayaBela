The only formula this offer uses — monthly, recurring, before VAT
Monthly subscription (ETB, before VAT) = (total students × 70%) × chosen rate

1) Take St. Joseph’s total enrolled students (A).
2) 30% of A is the discount / off — those students are not charged.
3) 70% of A is the payable students (B). Round to the nearest whole student.
4) Monthly payment before VAT = B × the pack rate (ETB 150 / 200 / 300 per payable student per month).
These pack rates are the lowest possible price in this offer. VAT is added later on the tax invoice if legally due. Besrate Gabriel School in Dire Dawa is 100% free of cost — do not put its students in (A).

3. The 30% off / 70% payable rule (St. Joseph)

From St. Joseph’s total enrolled students, this offer takes 30% off. That 30% is a discount — those students are not charged. The remaining 70% are the payable students. Every enrolled child may still use the system; the 30% is a commercial discount, not a login cap. Do not add Dire Dawa students into (A). Dire Dawa is 100% free (Section 6).

StepWhat to writeExample (St. Joseph 1,000 students, full pack)
1Total students (A)1,000
2Discount / off = A × 30%1,000 × 30% = 300 students not charged
3Payable students (B) = A × 70%1,000 × 70% = 700
4Choose monthly rate before VAT (C)ETB 300 (all 61 modules — lowest full-pack price)
5B × C = monthly subscription before VAT700 × 300 = ETB 210,000 per month, before VAT

Monthly subscription before VAT (ETB) = (St. Joseph total students × 70%) × chosen monthly rate

Check for the board: on a 1,000-student roll at the full pack, 210,000 ÷ 1,000 = ETB 210 per enrolled student per month, before VAT. The invoice line is still B × C. VAT is added on the tax invoice if Ethiopian law requires it. This amount is due every month for as long as the school stays subscribed.
