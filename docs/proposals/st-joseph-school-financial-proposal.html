3. The 30% student-count rule (St. Joseph only)

From St. Joseph’s total enrolled students, this offer bills 30% only. That 30% is the billable student base. The other 70% of the roll is not charged. Every enrolled child may still use the system; the 30% is a commercial base, not a login cap. Do not add Dire Dawa students into (A).

StepWhat to writeExample (St. Joseph 1,000 students, full pack)
1Total students (A)1,000
2A × 30% = …… (B)1,000 × 30% = 300
3Choose monthly rate (C)ETB 300 (all 61 modules)
4B × C = monthly subscription300 × 300 = ETB 90,000 per month

Monthly subscription (ETB) = (St. Joseph total students × 30%) × chosen monthly rate

Check for the board: on a 1,000-student roll at the full pack, 90,000 ÷ 1,000 = ETB 90 per enrolled student per month. The invoice still uses the 30% base (B × C). This amount is due every month for as long as the school stays subscribed.
