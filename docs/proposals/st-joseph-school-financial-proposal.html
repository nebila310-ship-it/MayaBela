3. The 30% billable-student rule

From the school’s total enrolled students, this offer bills 30% only. That 30% is the billable student base. The other 70% of the roll is not charged. Every enrolled child may still use the system; the 30% is a commercial base, not a login cap.

StepWhat to doExample (1,000 enrolled, full pack)
1Count enrolled students on the census date1,000
2Billable students = 30% of that count300
3Choose pack rateETB 300 (all 61 modules)
4Annual licence = billable × rate300 × 300 = ETB 90,000

Effective cost on the full roll, if the school takes the full pack: 90,000 ÷ 1,000 = ETB 90 per enrolled student per year. That figure is a check for the board; the invoice still uses the 30% base.

Each campus is counted on its own roll, then added:

Total annual licence = (30% × St. Joseph enrolment × rate) + (30% × Besrate Gabriel enrolment × rate)
