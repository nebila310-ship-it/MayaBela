1. Purpose and scope

This financial proposal is a one-time commercial offer of MaJo e-School Bridge to Lebawi International Academy, filtered to the academic scope in LIA Terms of Reference V3-1. The Academy purchases a licence to the 45 modules listed in Schedule A. Unit prices are copied from MaJo e-School Bridge Financial Proposal 2 (document MBT-LIA-FIN-2026-01).

The 45 TOR modules add to 3,155,000 ETB at those published unit prices. There is no 5,000 ETB deduction. The 500,000 ETB customization and go-live fee is excluded. VAT at 15% is then added.

2. What this proposal covers

  • One-time licence to each of the 45 TOR academic modules listed in Schedule A, at the published unit price.
  • Four login portals included with this pack: Administration/ERP, Teacher, Parent, and Student. They are not sold as modules.
  • The right to operate the selected modules on web and mobile app for Lebawi International Academy.

3. What this proposal does not cover

  • Finance and billing, procurement, inventory/store, and human resource management — TOR §3.2.
  • Payroll, live GPS, full transport operations, buses, driver register, CCTV, digital-operations desk, library, gallery, Maya Assistant, and system health — not required by TOR academic sections 5.1–5.21.
  • Subscription or SaaS fees; annual support retainers; recurring hosting, SMS, or payment-gateway charges.
  • Hardware, cameras, GPS devices, or school-owned equipment.
  • The Driver portal. It is offered only with a later transport pack, not with this academic TOR order.
  • The 500,000 ETB customization and go-live fee from Financial Proposal 2. It is excluded from this offer.

Recurring charges stay separate. Subscription, annual support, and hosting retainers are not in this document. They will be issued in a separate financial proposal, as stated in Financial Proposal 2.

4. Commercial conditions

  1. Minimum order remains 25 modules. This TOR pack is 45 modules and therefore qualifies.
  2. Each selected module is charged at its published unit price from Financial Proposal 2. There is no 5,000 ETB deduction.
  3. Four academic login portals are included. They are not sold as modules.
  4. The 500,000 ETB customization and go-live fee is excluded from this offer.
  5. This offer is valid for 90 days from 23 September 2026. Unit prices are VAT exclusive. VAT at 15% is added in Schedule B.

Financial Proposal 2 listed every shipped MaJo e-School Bridge module so a school could pick 25 or more. LIA’s TOR is academic only.
