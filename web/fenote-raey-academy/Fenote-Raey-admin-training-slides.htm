Who is in this room

Train the people who keep the books.

In the room

Owners, directors, principal/VP, registrar, student affairs, accountant, HR, store, transport head, IT helper.

Not this session

Classroom teachers, parents, students, drivers. They have their own dashboards and a later date.

Your job today

Sign in the right door, give the right duty, enter the first records, approve what families may see.

Agenda

Four hours, including tea. Gold items never drop.

Five rules

Say these until the room can say them back.

  1. One School ID — Fenote Raey never mixes with another school.
  2. The right door — Owner = Administrator. Office = Administration Staff. Not the Teacher tile.
  3. Duty, not curiosity — hide modules that are not the job.
  4. Parents see published truth — draft marks and unapproved links stay inside.
  5. WhatsApp is not the book — announcements, fees, attendance, discipline live here.

Two doors

A shorter sidebar is success.

Owner / Administrator

Every module. Only this person grants Full Access. Enroll authenticator after first sign-in.

Administration Staff

Only the branch you assigned: registrar, accountant, HR, store, transport, QA. Digital operations is help, not Full Access.

If everyone has Full Access, you have no security and no audit you can trust.

Catalog

The office ERP is grouped by duty.

Organization

School, campus, institution, CCTV.

Academics

Classes, timetable, markbook, grade approvals, report cards, attendance, at-risk, curriculum.

Student services

Students, admissions, alumni, parent-link, transfers, discipline, safeguarding.

Finance & HR

Fees, inventory, staff directory, leave, payroll, buses.

Communication

Announcements, calendar, gallery, messages — one voice.

System

Role permissions, audit, digital operations, go-live, backups, settings.

Identity

School, campus, cameras.

  • School Management = name and logo parents see
  • Campus = sites and rooms
  • Institution = licences and visits (leadership)
  • CCTV = existing cameras, staff only
People

HR creates the login. Roles hide the rest.

Add Administration Staff

Name, duty, username. First password from the owner/HR. They change it in Profile.

Role Permissions

Registrar, accountant, HR, store, transport head, QA, principal. Full Access stays locked to the owner.

Classroom teachers are a different tile and a different training day.

Students

Name, grade, class, date of birth → STU-####.

  • Add Student for one child
  • Go-live → Import for Excel/CSV
  • Duplicates skip — they are not grades
  • Write the Student ID down for the parent
Parents

They request. You recognise the child. You approve.

Parent needs School ID + Student ID + real date of birth.

Until Fenote Raey taps Approve, they see nothing.

Approve only people you know. This queue is the front door of the family app.

The grade sentence

Teacher enters → leadership approves → parent sees.

Markbook

One weighted book. No second Excel.

Grade approvals

Principal/VP queue. Drafts never leave the school.

Report cards

Publish the class set, then PDF/print.

At-risk = weak marks plus absence. Not a character score.

Money and store

Fees, stock, and pay slips leave a trail.

Finance

Charges, receipts, balances. Parents see their child only.

Inventory

Request → approve → receive → issue.

Payroll

Ethiopian income tax and pension on the slip. Do not run pay in the training lab.

One voice

If it is not in Announcements, it is a rumor.

  • Announcements — school or class
  • Calendar and events
  • Gallery — never safeguarding
  • Messages — office threads, not gossip
Transport and campus

Office assigns. Drivers and cameras are staff tools.

Buses / Live GPS

Parents see “live” only while the driver is sharing. Driver training is another day.

CCTV

Existing campus cameras. Staff only. Owner policy decides who may watch.

Friday ritual

Ten minutes. Owner in the room. MaJo only if nobody can log in.

  • Go-live → Backups
  • MFA help for staff who lost a phone
  • Excel leftovers
  • Optional JSON copy on a school PC
  • Settings → language: English, Amharic, Afaan Oromo
Lab

You click these six things before you leave.

  1. Ctrl+Shift+R · School ID · Administration Staff
  2. Open Students and find a class
  3. Add Student (or fill the form without saving if the owner forbids dummy data)
  4. Open the parent-link queue — do not approve real parents
  5. Post “Office training — ignore”, then remove it
  6. Owner opens Role Permissions — no Full Access gifts
