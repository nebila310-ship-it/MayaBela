3. Five rules — say them twice (opening and closing)

  1. One School ID. Fenote Raey’s records never mix with another school.
  2. The right door. Owners sign in as Administrator. Office staff sign in as Administration Staff. Classroom teachers have a different tile — not today.
  3. Duty, not curiosity. A registrar does not need payroll. Role Permissions hide what is not their job.
  4. Parents see published truth. Draft marks, draft discipline, and unapproved parent links stay inside the school.
  5. WhatsApp is not the book. Announcements, fees, attendance, and discipline live here so an argument has a record.

7. Block scripts — what you click and what you say

A. School identity (4 minutes)

Click School Management (and Campus if they have more than one building).

“This name and logo are what parents see at login. If it is wrong, they will not trust the fees page.”

B. People (8 minutes)

Click Human Resource → Add Administration Staff → Role Permissions.

  1. Show an existing staff row: name, role, login username.
  2. Assign Registrar or Accountant — not Full Access.
  3. Say first passwords come from HR/owner. Staff change them in Profile.

C. One student (10 minutes)

Click Students → Add Student.

  1. Required: full name, grade, class, date of birth. System gives STU-####.
  2. Write the new Student ID on the board. “This is what the parent will type.”
  3. Show Go-live → Import for Excel/CSV: name, grade, class, date of birth. Preview, then import. Duplicates skip.
Excel import does not write grades or exams. Do not promise “upload the old mark sheet and we are done.”

D. Parent link (6 minutes)

Click Parents & Link Approvals.

“The parent never guesses. They need School ID, Student ID, and the real date of birth. You approve only when you recognise the child. Until you approve, they see nothing.”

E. The grade rule (8 minutes)

Click Weighted Markbook then Examinations & Grade Approvals then Report Cards.

“Teachers type in Markbook. You do not keep a second spreadsheet. Leadership approves. Then report cards and the parent portal show the same number. If a parent saw a mark you have not approved, that is a process failure — call MaJo.”

F. Attendance and at-risk (5 minutes)

Click Attendance then At-risk & insights.

“A child is at-risk when weak marks sit on top of absence. The system is not guessing character.”

G. Money (8 minutes)

Finance → fee list. Inventory → stock or PR. Payroll → open, do not process.

“Fees, stock, and pay slips leave a trail. Ethiopian tax and pension are in payroll — your accountant still owns the law, the software does the arithmetic you configured.”

H. Speak once (5 minutes)

Click Announcements → New. Audience: parents (and a class if you have one). Publish.

“If it is not here, it is a rumor. Homeroom weekly letters also live on this desk later — not on four WhatsApp groups.”

I. CCTV and buses (4 minutes)

CCTV — staff only. Transport / Buses — office assigns; drivers have their own dashboard another day.

Do not show a live camera wall to the whole room if students are identifiable and visitors are present. Owner policy first.

J. Backups and go-live (8 minutes)

Click Go-live & compliance.

  1. Backups: school snapshot. Friday ritual. Older than 24 hours = reminder, not a product SLA.
  2. Also keep a JSON copy on a school PC if that is Fenote Raey policy.
  3. Sign-off: live dry-run + confirm production backup exists.
  4. Digital operations: who owns which laptop and driver phone.
“MaJo joins only if the whole school cannot log in. Friday ten minutes is yours: backup, MFA help, import leftovers.”

8. Hands-on lab (they must click)

Pair people. Owner watches. Each pair does:

  1. Hard-refresh, enter School ID, sign in as Administration Staff.
  2. Find Students. Search one real class (read only if they are nervous).
  3. Add the dummy student you prepared — or skip write if the owner forbids training data. Then at least open Add Student and fill without saving.
  4. Open Parent-link queue. Explain Approve.
  5. Post one training announcement titled “Office training — ignore” and delete/unpin after.
  6. Open Role Permissions as owner only. Point, do not grant Full Access.
Do not let them change payroll, publish report cards, or approve real parent links during training.
