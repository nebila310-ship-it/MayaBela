1. Recitals

WHEREAS Fenote Raey Academy wishes to purchase, on a one-time basis, a complete school operating system covering academics, student records, operations, parent engagement, transport, and institutional governance; and

WHEREAS MaJo Bridge Technologies and Events PLC is the developer of the MaJo Bridge Smart School Operating System (also demonstrated at https://mayabela.pages.dev) and will install, configure, train, and support the School; and

WHEREAS the Parties have agreed a lump-sum price of ETB 2,550,000.00 including VAT, with a first payment of ETB 400,000.00 including VAT on signing, and the remaining balance paid in four equal terms of ETB 537,500.00 including VAT, each due every four (4) months after the previous payment;

NOW, THEREFORE, the Parties agree as follows.

2. Nature of the Sale

This is a one-time sale, not a monthly or yearly software subscription. The Contract Price covers: (a) a perpetual licence for Fenote Raey Academy to use the System for its own school operations; (b) installation and configuration; (c) training; and (d) support and production hosting for twelve (12) months from Go-Live, as set out in Clause 11. No further licence fee is due for the software itself after the Contract Price is paid in full.

11. Support, hosting & warranty (included in the one-time price)

  • Warranty: ninety (90) days from Go-Live for defect repair on the included Scope (reproducible faults not caused by misuse, unauthorized change, or third-party outage).
  • Support (included): twelve (12) months from Go-Live, business hours Monday–Saturday 09:00–18:00 EAT, phone/email/in-app; critical outage target within eight (8) business hours.
  • Hosting (included): production hosting of the Fenote Raey tenant for the same twelve (12) months.
  • After month 12: the perpetual licence continues. Hosting and support after that year may be renewed by a short written addendum at a fee to be agreed; that is not a new software licence.
  • Security: school-scoped access, authenticated sessions, least-privilege roles. The Provider shall not use School Data for unrelated commercial purposes.

12. Licence & intellectual property

  • Licence: non-exclusive, non-transferable, perpetual right for Fenote Raey Academy only to use the System for its own educational operations, effective on full payment.
  • IP: the platform, frameworks, and generic modules remain the exclusive intellectual property of MaJo Bridge Technologies and Events PLC.
  • School Data: owned exclusively by the School. The Provider processes it only to deliver this Agreement.
  • Export: on written request at the end of hosting, the Provider shall supply a reasonable export of core registries within fifteen (15) business days.

13. Confidentiality & data protection

Each Party shall keep the other Party’s non-public business and technical information confidential for three (3) years after this Agreement ends. The Provider shall apply appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect personal data and shall notify the School without undue delay of any confirmed personal-data breach affecting School Data.

14. Limitation of liability

Except for fraud, willful misconduct, or a confidentiality/data breach caused by the Provider’s proven negligence, the Provider’s aggregate liability under this Agreement shall not exceed the Contract Price actually paid by the School. Neither Party is liable for indirect or consequential loss (including lost profits) to the extent permitted by Ethiopian law.

15. Termination

  • Either Party may terminate for a material breach not cured within thirty (30) days of written notice.
  • Amounts for payment terms already due remain payable. Completed installation work is not refundable except where the Provider is in uncured material breach.
  • On termination after the data-export window, the Provider may disable School access except as required to preserve a lawful dispute.

16. Governing law

This Agreement is governed by the laws of the Federal Democratic Republic of Ethiopia. The Parties shall first try to settle amicably within fifteen (15) business days; failing that, disputes shall go to the competent courts in Addis Ababa, unless the Parties agree in writing to arbitration.

17. Entire agreement

Amendments and Change Orders are valid only if signed by authorized representatives of both Parties. This Agreement (including Annex A) is the entire agreement on this subject and replaces prior oral discussions.
